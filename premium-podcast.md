# Preset: Premium Cinematic Podcast

- Format: 9:16 for Reels or 16:9 for long-form.
- Frame the speaker cleanly; protect headroom and eye line.
- Use subtle punch-ins on emphasis and hold on important lines.
- Correct exposure and white balance; preserve natural skin.
- Create gentle subject/background separation.
- Use refined lower thirds and bold Malayalam title cards.
- Keep captions readable and away from the face.
- Prioritize clean, intimate dialogue audio.
- Avoid unnecessary desk microphones, props or background elements not present in the source.
