# Dialogue and Sound Design

## Priority
1. Dialogue intelligibility
2. Natural ambience
3. Music, if appropriate and allowed
4. Sound effects

## Dialogue
Reduce steady noise carefully. Apply EQ, compression and de-essing conservatively. Automate levels between phrases. Avoid pumping, metallic denoising and clipping.

## Music and effects
Respect “no background music”. Duck music under dialogue. Use whooshes, impacts and risers sparingly and synchronize them to meaningful visual events. Avoid abrupt starts/ends and effects that mask words.

## QC
Listen on headphones and phone speakers. Check the entire mix for clipping, sudden level changes, masked speech and processing artifacts.
