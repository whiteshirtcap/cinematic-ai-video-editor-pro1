# Preset: Malayalam Political Reels

- Format: 9:16, 1080×1920 by default.
- Opening: strong 1–3 second hook grounded in an actual spoken idea.
- Typography: bold Malayalam headline, clear phrase captions, selective keyword emphasis.
- Visual style: documentary/editorial, restrained palette, clean contrast.
- B-roll: relevant and accurately contextualized; distinguish illustrative visuals from documentary evidence.
- Motion: purposeful punch-ins, clean cuts, restrained transitions.
- Sound: dialogue first; no music if requested.
- Integrity: preserve exact meaning; never fabricate quotes, documents, statistics or footage.
