# Malayalam Typography and Kinetic Captions

## Font and shaping
Use an installed Malayalam-capable font. Candidates include Noto Sans Malayalam and Manjari. Test conjuncts, chillus and vowel signs in the actual editor and export renderer.

## Hierarchy
1. One short, bold main headline.
2. Optional supporting line.
3. Spoken captions in short natural phrases.
4. Selective emphasis words.

## Motion
- Clean fade/slide for standard captions.
- Scale, mask reveal or colour accent for key words.
- Kinetic build for the opening title.
- Smooth easing; consistent animation timing.
- Avoid random bounce, spin, flicker and constant movement.

## Readability checks
- Prefer 1–2 lines.
- Break at natural phrase boundaries.
- Keep sufficient contrast and screen time.
- Use subtle shadow, outline or backing plate on busy footage.
- Avoid covering the speaker's face or important visual information.
- Proofread every caption against the verified transcript.
