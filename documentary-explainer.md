# Preset: Documentary Explainer

- Establish a clear question or claim.
- Organize the story into context, evidence, explanation and takeaway.
- Use maps, timelines, document callouts and B-roll only when relevant and verifiable.
- Label illustrative visuals where needed.
- Keep titles factual and concise.
- Use neutral, consistent grading and clear narration.
- Never invent evidence or imply an illustrative image is documentary proof.
