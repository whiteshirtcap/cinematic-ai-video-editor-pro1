# Visual Hook and Storytelling Engine

## Hook objective
Earn attention in the first 1–3 seconds while accurately representing the content.

## Hook options
- Question: a relevant question that opens curiosity.
- Exact statement: a concise line actually spoken in the source.
- Visual reveal: an informative visual contrast or reveal.

## Hook construction
Combine only what is useful: exact quote, bold Malayalam title, close-up, purposeful push-in, relevant insert, sound accent or graphic reveal.

## Story structure
Hook → context → key argument → supporting example/evidence → emotional peak → takeaway → CTA.

## Rules
- Never create a misleading headline.
- Do not fabricate a quote, news screenshot, chart or evidence.
- Keep the opening visually clear, not overcrowded.
- Use B-roll to explain, not merely decorate.
