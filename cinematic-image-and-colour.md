# Cinematic Image, Studio Lighting and Colour

## Technical correction
Correct white balance and exposure first. Recover highlights and shadows carefully, reduce noise conservatively, then match shots.

## Studio-look treatment
- Preserve natural skin texture and identity.
- Create subtle subject/background separation.
- Control practical-light highlights.
- Avoid plastic skin, face reshaping, edge halos and fake blur.
- Simulated relighting is not a physical change to the original capture.

## Chroma key
Sample the real key colour, tune tolerance, refine hair edges, suppress spill, preserve clothing/skin and match the replacement background's perspective, exposure and colour temperature.

## Creative grading
Choose a look appropriate to the story: neutral documentary, warm cinematic, restrained teal/warm, dark editorial or brand palette. Protect skin tones, highlight detail and shadow texture. Avoid oversaturation and excessive clarity.
