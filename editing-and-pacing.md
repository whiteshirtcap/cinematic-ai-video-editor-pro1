# Editing and Pacing

- Watch all footage before selecting the best moments.
- Build a beat sheet before detailed motion work.
- Use jump cuts to remove technical dead air, not meaningful pauses.
- Use J-cuts/L-cuts to connect related ideas naturally.
- Use punch-ins at meaningful emphasis, not every sentence.
- Vary shot scale to maintain visual rhythm.
- Keep transitions motivated by scene or idea changes.
- Preserve continuity and the speaker's real meaning.
- Keep original media and a reversible project version.
