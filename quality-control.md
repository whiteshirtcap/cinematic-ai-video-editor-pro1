# Final Quality Control

Review the full render from start to finish.

## Speech and captions
- No missing, duplicated or invented words.
- Names, numbers and Malayalam spelling checked.
- Captions synchronized and readable.
- Malayalam glyphs render correctly.

## Picture
- No accidental black frames, freezes or gaps.
- No stretched image, unwanted crop or flicker.
- No chroma-key halos or identity distortion.
- B-roll and titles match the spoken meaning.
- Exposure and colour are consistent.

## Sound
- Dialogue is clear and not clipped.
- Music/effects do not mask speech.
- No abrupt audio cuts or unwanted noise.

## Delivery
- Correct aspect ratio, resolution and frame rate.
- Confirm file exists and plays.
- Report actual export specs and known limitations.
